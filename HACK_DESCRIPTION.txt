## Build + submission

**Teams:** up to 4 people. Solo builders are welcome, and the event is designed to make it easy to meet potential teammates on the day. Every team member must be individually registered and approved

**Build:** projects should be created during the hackathon. You can bring ideas, use open-source libraries, APIs, templates and normal boilerplate, but the core product and functionality should be built this weekend.

**Submit:** submissions are due by **Sunday 26 July at 12:00 BST**. The submission link/process will be shared by organisers during the event.

**Be demo-ready:** have a working product, a clear problem/value statement, a public or accessible repo/link if requested, and a short demo path that judges can understand quickly.

## The brief

Build a consumer health product that genuinely helps humanity — in one weekend. Super technical or beautifully simple — early disease detection with wearables, a better care navigator, anything in between. Perfect one small thing.

**Seven strict rules:**

1. Build something NEW — don't bring what you already built, start fresh today.
2. Teams of 4 MAX. Going solo is allowed.
3. Doesn't have to be full-stack — perfecting one small, underserved problem often wins.
4. Demos are 1 MINUTE MAX — an MP4 video. PDFs fine for supporting material.
5. Submit GitHub repo + demo video by SUNDAY 12:00 GMT. No late entries — submit early.
6. Get REAL users honestly and show your evidence. Cheating scores ZERO.
7. Sponsor tools earn bonus points — use them where they genuinely help.

## What judges score

Four scores out of 10, plus two bonuses:

1. **IDEA /10** — novel, cool, needed. Perfect one small problem beats fixing everything.
2. **DESIGN /10** — AI design is recognizable now. Stand out: beautiful UX/UI that makes sense for the user.
3. **CODE QUALITY /10** — no slop. Technical work that pushes the AI tools to their limit scores here.
4. **DEMO /10** — 1 minute max. Clear, easy to understand?
5. **BONUS: real users** — bring evidence of HOW you got them (Reddit? called your mum's friends? tell us). Cheating scores ZERO.
6. **BONUS: sponsor tools used well** — only where they genuinely help.
