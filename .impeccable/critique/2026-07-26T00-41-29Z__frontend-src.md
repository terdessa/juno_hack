## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not slop.** A category-fluent user would trust this. The hard things are done: no modals anywhere (four unused dialog primitives prove the restraint was active), skeletons not spinners, and every animation frozen at a communicative frame under `prefers-reduced-motion` rather than switched off.

Ban check: display fonts, fluid clamp type, decorative colour, decorative motion, side-stripe borders, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical card grids, modal-first — all pass. Two fail: **uppercase tracked eyebrows** on the patient contact block, and **inconsistent component vocabulary**.

**Deterministic scan:** 1 finding across 90 files — `overused-font` on Geist (`__root.tsx:107`). Stylistic, contested, and in tension with the design review, which praised the one-family decision. Not a defect.

## Overall Impression

This is a well-argued system that doesn't fully obey its own rules. The reasoning in `styles.css` and the component comments is better than most shipped design systems — and three of the most load-bearing claims in those comments are not true of the code. The type scale says it replaced the eyeballed sizes; it was added alongside them. The colour comment says `--overdue` carries "an overdue follow-up"; nothing computes overdue-ness. The `--input` comment claims ~3:1; it measures 2.88:1.

The single biggest opportunity is ten lines: derive overdue-ness and let the interface show it.

## What's Working

**Modal discipline, applied for stated reasons.** Every consequential action is an inline confirm that keeps its context readable. `VoiceDock` is deliberately non-modal — "a thing on the desk, not a door" — because a GP mid-clinic is always deciding *about* something on screen.

**Reduced motion as a design problem, not a switch.** `pulse-dot` holds at 0.3 opacity, `mic-ring` at 0.35, `eq-bar` at `scaleY(0.7)`. Because every animation encodes state, `animation: none` would delete information.

**Empty states that teach the rules.** The inbox explains its *filtering rule* — "routine calls don't" land here — which is the only thing that lets a doctor trust an empty inbox.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] `failed` is labelled "No answer" and has no retry

A dispatch that never reached the network, an undiallable number, and a patient ignoring the phone all render as "No answer" — the patient's fault. There is no retry affordance on a failed call, so the task is silently dropped. In a product whose promise is "nothing forgotten", this is the one state that breaks the promise while looking like it worked. Every patient row currently holds a `+4400000000xx` placeholder, so it is also the state most likely to appear live.

**Fix:** rename to "Didn't get through"; surface the stored `error` on the detail page; add a `failed` branch reusing the existing confirm→dispatch path so it can be retried.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify` then `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] `--overdue` is a documented principle with no implementation

`PRODUCT.md` names "quiet, overdue and easily-forgotten cases surface on their own" as a principle. `styles.css` lists an overdue follow-up as one of four things allowed to carry hue. Verified: the token is never used for it, and no date comparison for past-due exists anywhere. A call due last Tuesday and one due next Tuesday are the same grey dot, the same word, the same sort position.

**Fix:** `const isOverdue = t.status === "queued" && new Date(t.scheduledAt) < new Date()`. Sort overdue first, give `StatusDot` a fifth branch, replace the status word with `Overdue · {formatRelative(...)}`.
**Command:** `/impeccable craft overdue surfacing`

### [P2] Three type sizes inside a 2px band

Verified counts across `routes/` + `components/medley/`: `text-sm` 63, `text-micro` 32, `text-body` 30, `text-xs` 17. `text-sm` is the most-used size in the app and is not in the design system. One list row in `/calls` uses five sizes.

**Fix:** mechanical sweep to the named scale, add a `--text-title` step for the two arbitrary hero sizes, then a CI grep banning bare `text-sm`/`text-xs` in those directories.
**Command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] ⌘K opens a window it never focuses

The dock renders after `<main>`, so focus stays behind it — the doctor tabs through the whole page to reach the composer of the window they just summoned. Escape isn't wired either. The headline shortcut of a product whose premise is "speaking beats filling in" is mouse-only.

**Fix:** focus the composer on mount, wire Escape to close, return focus to the rail button, add `role="dialog"` without `aria-modal`.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] Meaning-bearing copy below AA

`text-muted-foreground/70` ≈ 2.5:1 on the sentence "Dials itself, unattended, at the time shown" — the one line explaining that Medley phones a patient without the doctor present. `--input` measures 2.76–2.88:1 against the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 requires of a control boundary.

**Fix:** drop the `/70` (plain is 6.5:1 and still quiet); `--input: oklch(0.655 0 0)` light and `oklch(0.505 0 0)` dark — measured at 3.04:1 and 3.02:1.
**Command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**GP mid-clinic, 90 seconds** — Opens `/calls` to answer "what have I missed?" Six filter chips, no overdue signal, no meaningful sort: they must read every row and do date arithmetic. The core scan the product exists for is the one it can't serve. Hits ⌘K, the window appears, the cursor is still behind it, they reach for the mouse. Glances at `/calendar`, where `StatusDot` appears with a time and a name but no status word — the one view where colour is the sole carrier is the densest one.

**Screen-reader user** — `PatientPeek` sets `aria-describedby` to a tooltip containing name, age, NHS number, condition, the full medication list and three lines of notes; every arrow-key move reads a paragraph. Below 1024px that content is `hidden` while still being referenced. The home transcript is not a live region, so a streaming reply arrives silently.

**Keyboard-only user** — The dock can be opened by keyboard, cannot be focused, closed, or moved by keyboard; it is `onPointerDown`/`onPointerMove` only. If it settles over content there is no recovery. Credit where due: the decline ✕ correctly rises to full opacity on `focus-visible`, not just hover.

## Minor Observations

- **Dark mode is 50 lines of dead tokens** — fully specified, hue-shifted, never activated. No toggle, no `prefers-color-scheme`, no `classList` write. Wire it or delete it.
- 45 unused shadcn components; `button.tsx` unused while its markup is hand-copied 8×. This is why the vocabulary drifted.
- `text-reading` sets `line-height: 1.65` and is overridden by `leading-snug` at its call sites — the considered line-height is cancelled where it's used.
- `--live` (25°) and `--flag` (18°) are 7° apart; as 8px dots they are near-indistinguishable.
- `h-[calc(100vh-8rem)]` hardcodes header height; the conversation overflows when the live-call strip is showing.
- Three content widths across four routes (980px, 768px, 672px), none from a token.
- `console.error` in the root error component, which the repo's own style rule bans.

## Questions to Consider

1. If `--overdue` isn't wired to anything, is "equal attention by default" a principle or a paragraph? What else in `PRODUCT.md` is asserted rather than built?
2. Ink-as-primary correctly kills the cream anti-reference — but strip the four clinical hues and this is greyscale. Is the honest read that there is no accent, and that "colour means clinical state" is the rationale for not having chosen one?
3. The confirm before a call is the best thing in this product. Why does the reassurance stop the instant the phone starts ringing?
4. Every route teaches on empty except `/` — the most-visited one, which carries the promise. Should home be the agent, or the standing proof that the agent is holding something?
5. `VoiceDock` is "a thing on the desk, not a door." Does the metaphor survive the fact that half the users can only look at it?
